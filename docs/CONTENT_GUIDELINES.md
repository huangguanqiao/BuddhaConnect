# Editorial Writing Guide

## Purpose and tone
Write clear, respectful, approachable Buddhist educational content. Prefer plain Chinese for Chinese-language articles. Explain technical terms on first use; avoid sensational claims, proselytizing, and unsupported promises of spiritual or medical outcomes.

## Suggested structure
- Precise, reader-friendly title
- Short introduction describing the question addressed
- Clear sections with explanatory headings
- Examples clearly labeled as modern analogies where applicable
- Summary and optional further reading
- Verified source notes when claims depend on canonical texts

## Article requirements
- Check existing articles for duplication.
- Distinguish (1) canonical text, (2) traditional interpretation, (3) editorial explanation.
- Attribute tradition-specific positions; do not present one school's view as universal.
- No fabricated quotes or page, sutra, chapter or verse numbers.
- Original editorial prose only unless quotation and licensing permit otherwise.
- Avoid copying long passages from external sites.
- Use internal links only after confirming their routes exist.
- Drafts should be reviewed by a human before merging.

## Current content format
Articles live in `src/content/articles/*.md` with required `title`, `description`, `date` and optional `category`. See docs/PUBLISHING.md. Do not add tags, language, status or other fields without implementing schema support.
