# BuddhaConnect site-wide artwork
These original vector assets were created for the BuddhaConnect website and are maintained in Git.
- `lotus-mark.svg`: brand lotus motif for header.
- `hero-mountains.svg`: site homepage sunrise landscape.
They are website-level decorative assets, not article images. SVG avoids binary dependencies, is scalable and version-controlled. Do not imply historical or religious authenticity. The artwork is original to this project; no external photos or third-party copyrighted imagery are embedded.
